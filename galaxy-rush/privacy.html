PRIVACY POLICY

Privacy information for our Android space-combat game

Offline • Single-player • No in-game ads
Effective date: October 21, 2026 · Developer: NORM INTERACTIVE

01Introduction

NORM INTERACTIVE respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how Galaxy Rush: Space Combat (the “Game”) handles information when you use the Android application.

The Game is designed primarily as an offline, single-player experience. It does not require account registration or an internet connection to play.

02Information Stored on Your Device

The Game stores information locally to provide gameplay features and remember preferences, including:

  • Player names or nicknames voluntarily entered for local high scores.
  • Scores, completed waves, defeated enemies, combo records, and random record identifiers.
  • Language, music and sound volume, touch-control preferences, and music playback settings.

These records use Unity PlayerPrefs and Android SharedPreferences and are not separately encrypted by the Game. You may use a nickname instead of your real name.

03Data Collection and Sharing

The reviewed version does not intentionally transmit player names, high scores, gameplay statistics, or preferences to servers operated by NORM INTERACTIVE.

The Game has no online accounts or cloud leaderboards, and no integrated third-party analytics service was identified as actively transmitting gameplay data. Certain Android system services may process information independently, as described below.

04Android System Services

Backup and restoration: Depending on device settings, Android may include Game preferences and records in system backups or transfers to another device. Backup storage, restoration, and deletion are governed by the applicable provider.

Emoji and font support: Android's system font provider may obtain emoji resources. The Game does not intentionally include player names or scores in font requests.

Keyboard: High-score names are entered using your selected Android keyboard. Independent processing by that keyboard is governed by its provider and your device settings.

05Advertising and Analytics

The current version does not display advertisements. No active advertising SDK, Firebase integration, or cloud-based gameplay analytics transmission was identified in the reviewed release.

If these features are introduced later, this policy will be updated as appropriate.

06Data Retention and Deletion

Local Game information remains until replaced, cleared, or removed. To clear it, use Android Settings → Apps → Galaxy Rush: Space Combat → Storage → Clear Data (wording may vary).

Uninstalling the Game generally removes local application data. Previously created Android system backups may remain subject to the backup provider's retention and deletion practices and may be restored later.

07Data Security

The Game relies on Android's application sandbox and operating-system security mechanisms to protect locally stored information.

The Game does not provide separate encryption for locally stored player names, scores, or preferences.

08Children's Privacy

The Game does not require account creation or intentionally request submission of personal information to NORM INTERACTIVE.

Players may use a nickname for local high scores. The reviewed version contains no advertisements or social communication features.

09Changes to This Policy

NORM INTERACTIVE may update this Privacy Policy when Game functionality, data practices, or applicable requirements change.

Updated versions will be published on this page with a revised effective date.
